## Limitations

- Skeleton branch has never seen a RealSense + RTMPose skeleton (public Kinect / motion-capture only). Results do not describe the Clinic Station until validated on team recordings.
- Camera + strap together was not measured on real people: no source records both. Needs team recordings with the straps worn.
- Strap branch trained only on a wrist-worn Apple Watch (SPAR). It does not transfer to BNO085 straps on the arm, forearm and chest; the hub ignores strap input until validated.
